Definitions. Late: delivered after the estimated delivery date. Excess days: days past the estimate. Expected rate: the platform late rate for each order's month and destination state, summed over a seller's orders. Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals.

Data. Olist Brazilian e-commerce public dataset, delivered orders purchased January 2017 to August 2018. Pipeline: SQL marts in SQLite, Python for validation, bootstrap and simulation. Built by Sidhant Pujara.
